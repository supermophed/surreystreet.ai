Summary: Surreystreet AI collects information you provide to deliver property management services through Yuki Bird. We use your mobile number to send SMS notifications you have opted into. We do not sell your personal information. California residents have specific rights under CCPA described in Section 6.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use Yuki Bird property management services or sign up for SMS notifications:

  • Identity information: Full name, email address
  • Contact information: Mobile phone number, mailing address associated with your property
  • Property information: Property address, access preferences, guest information you provide through the Yuki Bird system
  • Communication data: SMS messages sent to and from the Yuki Bird number, timestamps, delivery status
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited on surreystreet.ai, time and date of visits

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver SMS property notifications you have opted in to receive
  • Operate and improve the Yuki Bird property management service
  • Communicate with you about your property, guests, and service updates
  • Respond to your requests, questions, and support needs
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal effects without human oversight.

3. SMS messaging program

Surreystreet AI operates an SMS notification program on behalf of Yuki Bird Properties. By opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages related to your property management service, including:

  • Security and access alerts (doorbell events, motion detection, lock activity)
  • Guest communication relays
  • Property maintenance and status updates
  • Responses to your inbound SMS queries

Message frequency: Varies based on property activity. Typically 2–10 messages per week.
Charges: Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details.
To opt out: Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one final confirmation and no further messages.
For help: Reply HELP or email vevina@surreystreet.ai

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any property management service. Opting out will not affect your ability to use Yuki Bird services through other channels.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Twilio Inc.: Our SMS delivery provider, bound by data processing agreements.
  • Supabase Inc.: Our database provider for storing session and property data, bound by data processing agreements.
  • Anthropic PBC: We use Claude AI to process and respond to inbound messages. Message content may be processed by Anthropic's systems.
  • Yuki Bird Properties: As the property management entity on whose behalf we operate.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of persons or property.

5. Data retention

  • SMS opt-in consent records: 4 years (required for TCPA compliance)
  • Message logs: 12 months rolling
  • Account and property information: Duration of service relationship plus 2 years
  • Opt-out records: Indefinitely, to honor your opt-out preference

6. Your rights

All users

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact us at vevina@surreystreet.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale: We do not sell personal information. No action is required.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.
  • Right to correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.

To submit a CCPA request, email vevina@surreystreet.ai with the subject line "CCPA Request." We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days.

7. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your information.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by sending an SMS notification to opted-in users and by updating the effective date above. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
